### 4.2 City Page Framework
Every city page must include:
- Unique H1 and intro paragraph referencing city.
- 600-1200 words unique content (no duplication).
- Services available in that city.
- Project highlights from that city/nearby.
- 2-4 localized FAQs.
- CTA block with local intent copy.
- LocalBusiness + ServiceArea schema fields.
- Internal links to service pages and estimate form.

### 4.3 Internal Linking Blueprint
- Homepage links to all high-priority services and cities.
- Each service page links to 5-8 relevant city pages.
- Each city page links to 3-5 service pages.
- Blog posts link to 1 city page + 1 service page + estimate CTA.

---

## 5) On-Page SEO Standards

Per indexable page:
- Title: 50-60 chars, keyword + location + brand.
- Meta description: 140-160 chars, benefit + CTA.
- Exactly one H1.
- Logical H2/H3 structure.
- Descriptive alt text for all key images.
- Canonical URL set.
- Open Graph and Twitter tags.
- Schema markup when applicable.

Sample metadata pattern:
- Title: Interior Painting in Orlando, FL | Vini Painting
- Description: Professional interior painting in Orlando with clean prep, premium finishes, and fast turnaround. Request your free estimate today.

## 6) Local SEO (Google Business Profile)

### 6.1 GBP Setup Checklist
- Primary category: Painter
- Secondary categories: Painting, House Painter, Contractor (as applicable)
- Service areas configured to target city cluster.
- Business description with location/service keywords.
- Full NAP consistency with website.
- Opening hours + holiday updates.
- Services list mirrored from website.
- Weekly photo uploads (projects, team, before/after).

### 6.2 GBP Posting Cadence
- 2 posts/week:
  - Recent project showcase
  - Offer/seasonal message
  - Educational tip

### 6.3 Review Engine
- Ask every completed customer for Google review.
- 24h and 7-day follow-up sequence.
- Reply to all reviews within 48h.
- Tag positive reviews by city/service and reuse on website testimonials.

## 9) Technical SEO Requirements

Must-have implementation:
- Dynamic XML sitemap (pages, posts, cities, services, gallery).
- robots.txt with clean directives.
- Schema JSON-LD injected server-side.
- Core Web Vitals optimization.
- Image optimization (WebP/AVIF + lazy load).
- 301 redirects manager in admin.
- 404 monitoring and fix workflow.
- Breadcrumb schema.
- FAQ schema where relevant.

Performance targets:
- LCP < 2.5s
- INP < 200ms
- CLS < 0.1
- Mobile Lighthouse >= 90
